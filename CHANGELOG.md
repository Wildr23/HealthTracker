# Changelog

## v1.4.0 — Watch Activities, Unified Connected Devices, Closed-App Scale FGS & 11-Metric Body Analytics
- **Watch Activities, Steps & Distance (`WatchActivityRepository.kt`, `WatchActivityScreen.kt`, `WatchDailyBarChart`):** Reads 30-day daily steps, distance (`READ_DISTANCE`), and active calories via Health Connect (`OHealth` / `HealthConnectDataSource`), plus non-gym watch sessions (outdoor/treadmill running, walking, hiking, cycling, swimming, stairs, HIIT, yoga) deduplicated against logged strength workouts (`WatchActivityDeduplicator`). Surfaced via a clickable `Today` OHealth card (with daily step-goal progress bar), a shortcut card in `Gym -> Progress`, and a full `WatchActivityScreen` (`Routes.WATCH_ACTIVITY`) with `7D/14D/30D` bar charts and filterable cardio feed.
- **Unified Connected Devices Hub (`ConnectedDevicesScreen.kt`, `SettingsScreen.kt`):** Centralized Smartwatch / Health Connect permissions (including `Distance` & editable daily step goal) and Xiaomi Smart Scale settings + Samsung Battery Unrestricted check into `Settings -> Connected Devices` (`Routes.CONNECTED_DEVICES`), removing bulky setup banners from `Health` while keeping a 1-tap weigh-in icon in `Health`'s `TopAppBar`.
- **Reliable Closed-App Xiaomi Scale Capture (`XiaomiScaleCaptureService.kt`, `XiaomiScaleBleController.kt`, `ScaleScanWatchdogWorker.kt`):** Upgraded hardware `PendingIntent` background scan to `SCAN_MODE_BALANCED`, added a short `connectedDevice` foreground burst service (`SCAN_MODE_LOW_LATENCY`, 25s max / 7s bioimpedance window) launched on scale wake frames, added a 6-hour WorkManager watchdog (`ScaleScanWatchdogWorker`), `REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS` UI & diagnostic counters (`scaleLastWakeEpochSec`, `scaleIgnoredFamilyCount`), and set default `birthYear = 1993`.
- **Interactive 11-Metric Body Composition Analytics (`BodyMetricSeries.kt`, `BodyCompositionMetricChart`, `HealthScreen.kt`):** Full switchable time-series analytics for all 11 Xiaomi body metrics (`Weight`, `Fat %`, `Muscle Mass`, `Lean Body Mass`, `Water %`, `Protein %`, `Visceral Fat`, `Bone Mass`, `BMI`, `BMR`, `Metabolic Age`), date range pills (`14D..All`), optional weight comparison overlay, smart good/bad delta coloring, clickable `ScaleCompositionDashboardCard` tiles, and enriched history log.
- **Supabase Cloud Sync (`SupabaseHttpClient.kt`, `CloudSyncManager.kt`, `CloudSyncWorker.kt`):** Compile-verified and integrated Supabase REST/Auth backup, restore, and 24-hour Free Tier heartbeat worker alongside v1.4.0 (`watch_activities` & `user_preferences_sync` support).
- **Verification:** Added `BodyMetricSeriesTest` and `WatchActivityDedupeTest` (28/28 unit tests passing across all 10 suites), verified `adb install -r` live UI QA on Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra (`versionName=1.4.0`, preserving Room `version = 1` database).

## v1.3.0 — Direct Xiaomi Smart Scale (`XMTZC05HM`) BLE Capture & Body Composition
- **Direct BLE Broadcast Capture (`XiaomiScaleBleParser.kt`, `XiaomiScaleBleController.kt`):** Intercepts Xiaomi Mi Body Composition Scale 2 (`0x181B`) and Mi Smart Scale (`0x181D`) BLE advertisements without needing Zepp Life open. Supports live foreground scanning with 2-stage stabilization + 6s bioimpedance wait fallback and 1-tap auto-binding.
- **Background Auto-Intercept & Family Weight Filter (`XiaomiScaleBleReceiver.kt`, `XiaomiScaleBootReceiver.kt`):** Hardware-offloaded `PendingIntent` BLE scan bound to your scale's MAC address (restored automatically on boot / package update / Bluetooth enable), filtering out weigh-ins outside your personal kg range (`±12 kg` default or custom min/max) so family weigh-ins are safely ignored, and posting a notification on capture.
- **Full Body Composition & Persistence (`XiaomiBodyCompositionCalculator.kt`, `ScaleReadingsRepository.kt`):** Computes `BMI`, `Body Fat %`, `Lean Body Mass`, `Muscle Mass`, `Water %`, `Protein %`, `Visceral Fat`, `Bone Mass`, `BMR`, `Metabolic Age`, `Ideal Weight`, and 9 Zepp Life `Body Type` classes; saves `kg` & `fatPercent` to `body_weights` (preserving Room `version = 1` and existing tape measurements) and full composition snapshots to DataStore.
- **UI & Profile:** Added live weigh-in & family/profile settings screen (`Routes.XIAOMI_SCALE`), OLED Body Composition Dashboard card in `Health -> Weight Chart`, enriched weight log rows, and quick access in `Health` & `Settings`.
- **Verification:** Added `XiaomiScaleEngineTest` (23/23 unit tests passing across 8 test suites) and verified v1.3.0 UI live on Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra (`adb install -r`).

## v1.2.0 — Anatomical BodyMap, Muscle Stimulus & Recovery, Jefit 3-Tab Exercise Detail
- **Anatomical Bézier 2D BodyMap (`BodyMap.kt`):** Smooth front + back anatomical silhouettes (`cubicTo`) over dark base anatomy, supporting dual `FOCUS` and `RECOVERY` color modes plus zoomed 3×4 `MuscleCategoryCircle` exercise filters.
- **Personalized Workout Muscle Stimulus % (`MuscleRecoveryEngine.kt`):** Per-exercise anatomical muscle shares and session Stimulus `%` (`0–150%`) computed relative to the user's own historical upper-half median weighted dose (`weight × reps × intensity` ignoring `WARMUP`/`CARDIO`), displayed alongside Focus `%` in `WorkoutSummaryScreen` and `BodyMapModalSheet`.
- **Muscle Recovery Status & Injured Muscles:** Dedicated `MuscleRecoveryScreen` (`Routes.MUSCLE_RECOVERY`) + preview cards on `TodayScreen` and `Gym -> Progress`, plus readiness `%` badges on `Routines` split day cards. Models chronological fatigue decay across 10 standard muscle groups with OHealth sleep adjustment and sore/injured muscle checkboxes persisted in DataStore (`UserPreferencesRepository`, Room DB kept untouched at `version = 1`).
- **Jefit-Style 3-Tab Exercise Detail (`Історія` | `Графік` | `Інфо`):** Default `Історія` tab with date cards and borderless 4-column `№ | Вага × повт. | Відп. | 1RM` tables highlighting best-set `1RM` in blue; `Графік` tab with `14Д..Усі` range pills, `1RM / Тоннаж` toggle, editable `1RM` Goal dialog with right-aligned pill badge & Emerald line on `StrengthProgressionChart`, `Мін./Макс.`, 5-PRs, Comeback, and `%` ladder; `Інфо` tab with anatomical BodyMap, `%` muscle shares, and profile metadata.
- **Verification & On-Device QA:** Added `MuscleRecoveryEngineTest` (19/19 unit tests passing) and visually verified live flows + Goal badge polish on Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra (`adb install -r`, preserving all 202 logged sessions).

## v1.0.0 — Core MVP (Milestone 1 + Milestone 2 Core)
- **Database & Catalog:** Room schema (`exportSchema = true`) covering workouts, exercises, sets, cached HR/calorie streams, computed metrics, body weights, and forward-compatible Phase 2–4 tables (`supplement_*`, `lab_*`, `daily_vitals`, `health_events`, `body_pain_logs`, `periodic_reports`), plus matching `supabase/schema.sql` with RLS. Seeded 51 exercises (`en` + `uk`) from `assets/exercises.json`.
- **Jefit CSV Importer:** Section-aware RFC-4180 parser handling multi-line routine descriptions, `lbs → kg` conversion with 0.25 kg rounding, `eid` 112/250 split rules, 40 external HC-duplicate cardio flagging, `PROFILE` outlier detection, Cyrillic notes, and idempotent upsert preserving cached Health Connect HR samples.
- **PR & 1RM Engine:** Epley/Brzycki/Lombardi + RPE-adjusted 1RM, 5-category PR tracking (`BEST_E1RM`, `MAX_WEIGHT`, `MAX_REPS_AT_WEIGHT`, `MAX_SET_VOLUME`, `MAX_SESSION_VOLUME`), live in-workout PR celebration, rep-max percentage ladder, Era comeback comparison, and interactive progression charts with >60-day training break compacting.
- **Smart Hybrid Calorie Engine:** 3-component calculation combining OHealth 1-minute buckets with biomechanical work (`TIER_1` / `TIER_2` / `TIER_3`), anaerobic MET cost, EPOC bonus, and pre-workout HR-blunting protection.
- **Health Connect Read-Only Gateway & Workers:** Core/history/background permission flow with rationale screen, dynamic OHealth source discovery, overlap session matcher, 20s HR interpolation + per-set peak HR / 60s recovery, `MergeWorker` backoff retries, `BackfillWorker`, and `WorkoutTimerService` foreground notification.
- **Bilingual UI (`en` + `uk`):** Full Jetpack Compose Material 3 app across Today, Gym (History/Routines/Exercises), Stack/Health/Insights previews + Body Weight log + weekly muscle volume chart, Active Workout logger, Workout Summary, Exercise Detail, Jefit Importer, and Settings (with JSON backup export/import).
