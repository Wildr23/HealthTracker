# Changelog

## v1.5.2 — Stack Multi-Dose, Cascade Fix, Reliable Reminders & Reference UX
- **Fixed Critical `ON DELETE CASCADE` Bug (`Daos.kt`, `SupplementRepository.kt`, `CloudSyncManager.kt`):** Replaced Room `OnConflictStrategy.REPLACE` (`upsertItem` / `upsertSchedule` / `upsertLog`) with explicit `insert` / `update` / `updateItemServings` so logging intake, restocking, or editing a supplement never triggers SQLite `DELETE` cascade that wiped schedules and intake history or pushed the supplement into the fallback afternoon slot.
- **Multi-Dose / Multi-Schedule Support (`SupplementScheduleEngine.kt`, `SupplementInteractionEngine.kt`, `StackScreen.kt`):** A single supplement can now hold 1–5 independent intake schedules (e.g. Omega-3 morning + evening, Creatine daily morning + post-workout) with per-dose weekday toggles, 24h Material 3 `TimePicker`, `logId → scheduleId` slot matching, accurate multi-dose remaining-days & streak/adherence math, and full multi-schedule compatibility checks.
- **Reliable Samsung / Android 15–16 Push Reminders (`StackReminderScheduler.kt`, `StackReminderReceiver.kt`, `AndroidManifest.xml`, `XiaomiScaleBootReceiver.kt`):** Added `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`, exact alarm `setExactAndAllowWhileIdle` scheduling up to 16 slots, lifecycle-aware `Notifications` + `Exact Alarms` permission banners, `IMPORTANCE_HIGH` channel (`stack_reminders_v2`) with `VISIBILITY_PUBLIC` and interactive `Taken ✓` & `+30m` Snooze actions on both real and test notifications, Next Scheduled Reminder indicator, and 1-tap `Test push (5s)` button.
- **Custom & Editable Intake Timestamps (`StackScreen.kt`, `StackViewModel.kt`, `SupplementRepository.kt`):** Added 24h Material 3 time-picker dialogs with quick relative presets (`Now`, `-30m`, `-1h`, `-2h`, `Scheduled`) to log supplements or caffeine at any custom time on **Today** and edit timestamps (or caffeine mg) for any existing **Today** or **History** log.
- **SuppCo / Apple Health / Medisafe UX Redesign (`StackScreen.kt`, `StackViewModel.kt`):** Rebuilt Stack into 3 tabs (`Today` | `My Stack` | `History`) with an above-the-fold chronological checklist, 6 natural time-slot group cards (`Morning`, `Afternoon`, `Workout`, `Evening`, `Bedtime`, `Anytime`), 1-tap `Take all ✓` batch logging with Undo snackbar, auto-collapsing completed slots, and haptic feedback.
- **Verification:** Added Room `SupplementRepository` cascade-regression & `updateLogTime` test in `GymTrackerDatabaseTest`, expanded `SupplementScheduleEngineTest` and `SupplementInteractionEngineTest`.
